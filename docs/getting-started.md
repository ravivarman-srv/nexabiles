# Getting started

Welcome to Nexabilis! Get up and running with your intelligent AI CRM in just a few minutes.

## 1. Create your account

Start by creating an account at the /signup page. 
After sign-in, you will land on your /dashboard.

The inbox, contacts, pipelines, automations, and broadcasts modules will be ready to use immediately, but they will be empty until you connect your integrations.

## 2. Connect WhatsApp

To start messaging your customers, you'll need to connect a WhatsApp Business number.
Follow the [WhatsApp setup](./whatsapp-setup.md) guide to connect your Meta account, get your access tokens, and verify your webhook.
